## 3. Reddit r/privacy, r/TravelHacks, r/privacytoolsIO (EN)

**Title:** I built a free tool that finds hidden cameras using just your phone's flash — physics, no gimmicks

**Body:**
Lens retro-reflection is the one physical signature basically every hidden camera
can't hide: shine a light and the objective glints back. My web app turns your
phone's flash into a pulsed illuminator and flags glints that blink in sync with
it (that's the key — random glossy surfaces don't sync).

It also has a second detector: if you own a cheap USB-C thermal camera
(InfiRay, Topdon, FLIR One — from ~$100) or a phone with a built-in one, it
analyses your thermal video for small persistent warm spots that indicate
powered electronics — while filtering out cooling coffee cups, radiators and
moving people. All processing is on-device, nothing gets uploaded.
